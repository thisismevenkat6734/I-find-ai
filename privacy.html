Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Welcome to I FIND AI. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit or use our website.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Text that you enter into the writing analysis tool.
  • Images or videos that you select for analysis.
  • Basic technical information required for website operation.

2. Uploaded Content

Our intended approach is privacy-first processing. Selected files may be processed locally in your browser where the relevant functionality supports it.

However, local processing is not guaranteed for every future detection feature. If a feature uses an external processing service, we will disclose that before you submit content to that service.

Important: Do not submit confidential, sensitive, personal, or legally protected content unless you understand how the specific feature processes it.

3. Data Storage

I FIND AI is initially designed without a permanent user-content database or cloud file storage. Temporary browser memory and browser-managed caches may be used for previews and website functionality.

This does not mean that every future feature or third-party service will operate without data storage. Any change will be reflected in this policy.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser storage for essential functionality, such as remembering whether the welcome screen has already appeared during the current session.

Advertising, analytics, or other third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies if introduced. Their use will be disclosed when applicable.

5. Third-Party Services

Some website features may rely on external services, including hosting providers, advertising networks, analytics providers, or content verification services.

Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their independent data practices.

6. Advertising

I FIND AI may display advertisements to support website operations.

If third-party advertising services are enabled, they may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings and laws.

7. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect the website and its users. However, no website or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

I FIND AI is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. Children should use the service only with appropriate parental or guardian guidance where required by law.

9. Your Choices

You can choose not to submit content for analysis. You can also clear browser data through your browser settings. This may remove locally stored website preferences and cached resources.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, technologies, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the official contact information published on the I FIND AI website.

Please do not send passwords, confidential documents, or sensitive personal information through general enquiries.
